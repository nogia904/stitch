Technical Specifications & Care

Constructed exclusively from certified solid European White Oak harvested from sustainably managed forests in Bavaria (FSC® C104829). Finished with organic, zero-VOC plant oils that enrich the timber grain over time.

Bouclé: Vacuum weekly with a low-suction upholstery attachment to prevent fine airborne dust from embedding into fiber loops. Blot spills promptly with a dry undyed cloth; avoid vigorous rubbing. Professional dry clean recommended for stubborn marks.

Oak Frame: Dust regularly with a microfiber cloth. Reapply organic beeswax or Danish oil once annually to nourish the exposed wood grain and maintain resistance to drying.
